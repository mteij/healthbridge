# Where the health numbers come from

Healthbridge shows estimates for orientation. They are not medical advice. Every rule below is
applied as written; "checked" means the figure was confirmed against the cited source.

| What | Rule | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max heart rate | 211 − 0.64 × age, raised when your own workouts show a higher value (second-highest workout maximum, so one sensor spike is ignored). Standard error about 11 bpm | Nes et al. 2013, HUNT Fitness Study, 3,320 healthy adults with verified maximal effort ([PubMed 22376273](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22376273/)) | checked. The paper found older formulas underestimate HRmax above age 30 |
| Intensity zones | Share of max heart rate: light < 64 %, moderate 64–76 %, vigorous 77–95 %, peak ≥ 96 % | ACSM Guidelines, 10th ed. (2018) ([table](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8581515/table/T1)) | moderate and vigorous bands checked; the other two limits follow the same ACSM table but were not re-checked |
| Weekly activity | Target 150 min; moderate minutes count once, vigorous and peak minutes twice (150–300 moderate ≙ 75–150 vigorous) | WHO 2020 guidelines ([PMC7719906](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7719906/)) | ranges checked |
| Daily steps | Goal 8,000 (under 60) or 6,000 (60 and over): the lower end of where mortality benefit levels off | Paluch et al. 2022, Lancet Public Health, 15 cohorts (DOI 10.1016/S2468-2667(21)00302-9) | the paper exists; the age ranges (60+: 6,000–8,000, younger: 8,000–10,000) come from secondary coverage and were **not confirmed in the paper** |
| Sleep | Target 7 hours or more for adults; no upper limit set | AASM and Sleep Research Society consensus 2015 ([PMC4434546](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4434546/)) | checked |
| BMI | Under 18.5, 18.5–24.9, 25–29.9, 30 and over | WHO adult classification ([WHO](https://www.who.int/data/nutrition/nlis/info/malnutrition-in-women)) | checked. Other cut-offs are used for some Asian populations; not applied here |
| Resting energy | 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5 (men) or − 161 (women) | Mifflin et al. 1990; Frankenfield et al. 2005 review found it the most reliable common equation, though individual error is real | checked |

Sleep stages are shown as measured, without a verdict: published normal ranges for deep and REM
sleep vary between sources and with age.
